# Tormentos da Arena Macabra — prompts de imagem

Prompts pra gerar os mapas da missão. Estão em inglês porque os geradores entendem melhor.

- **Pedaço fixo:** todo prompt de zona termina com o mesmo estilo. Assim os 12 mapas saem com a mesma cara.
- **Formato:** peça quadrado ou 4:3, o maior tamanho que o gerador der.
- **Grade:** "no grid", porque o VTT põe a grade por cima.

**Estilo (já vai no fim de cada prompt):**
> top-down orthographic battlemap for a tabletop RPG, seen directly from above, no perspective, no grid, no text, no labels, no UI, highly detailed hand-painted digital art, grim survival horror, Brazilian countryside at night, muted greens and browns with blood-red accents, wet ground, dried blood stains, subtle red fog, dramatic moody lighting, readable floor space for tokens

---

## 0. Mapa explicativo da Arena

Pra mostrar aos jogadores como a arena funciona. Os geradores erram texto, então peça sem letras e escreva os números e a legenda depois (no Canva, por exemplo). Se o gerador escrever bem, troque "no text" por "with the numbers 1 to 12".

> Top-down illustrated game board map of a deadly survival arena, a large rectangular compound divided into 12 walled zones arranged in a 4 by 3 grid, each zone separated by tall rusty corrugated metal walls topped with barbed wire and sharp scrap, with heavy iron gates connecting neighboring zones. Bottom row from left to right: a ruined stone chapel with a pointed bell tower and a small graveyard; a blood-red swamp with twisted dead trees and a rotten wooden footbridge over a dark river; an empty clearing in front of a huge crude exit gate made of rusted metal plates with a small generator and lever on the wall; an industrial machine house with a boiler and pipes. Middle row: a rusty military watchtower with two tents, barbed wire and a wrecked military jeep; overgrown stone ruins with a blood-stained stone altar in the center; a dark wooden hunter's cabin in a forest clearing (the main hatch to the underground tunnels is here, a round metal hatch on the ground); an old stone well with a rope and bucket next to a small wooden gazebo. Top row: the ruins of an old stone fort swallowed by vines with a dark stairway descending into dungeons; a second huge exit gate like the first one; a camping clearing with three tents (blue, yellow and dark green) around a dead campfire with log benches and red puddles; a small abandoned medical post with a cross on the roof. Faint dotted lines underground connecting hidden hatches in every zone, showing a tunnel network. Each zone has a small empty circle badge in a corner for a number. Style: aged parchment map pinned on a dark wooden table, ink drawings with watercolor washes, blood splatters, rusty chains and a bone saw at the edges of the frame, survival horror, clear and readable, no text, no letters.

**Legenda pra escrever depois:**
- **Desafios:** 1 Capela, 4 Casa de Máquinas, 9 Abatedouro, 12 Posto Médico (caveira);
- **Passagens:** 2 Pântano de Sangue, 5 Torre de Vigia, 6 Ruínas, 7 Cabana do Caçador, 8 Poço e Gazebo, 11 Acampamento;
- **Saídas:** 3 e 10 (portão);
- **Escotilha Principal:** zona 7 (círculo de metal);
- **Túneis:** as linhas pontilhadas.

## 1. Capela (Desafio)

> A ruined gothic stone chapel inside a walled courtyard, crooked iron gate covered in rust leading into a weedy yard with a few tilted tombstones; inside the long narrow nave, broken and rotting wooden pews pushed to the sides and stained with pools of blood, cracked dusty stained glass windows at the far end casting red, yellow and orange light on the stone floor, the central window shows a distorted saint with a hidden face and its top is broken open showing a red moon; three low steps up to the altar where four imposing throne-like chairs of metal and wood stand in a circle under a pulpit, cables, pipes and chains holding a suspended generator from the ceiling that bathes the thrones in red light. Sacred and profane. + estilo

## 2. Pântano de Sangue (Passagem)

> A flat swampy plain of uneven waterlogged ground covered by thick warm reddish mist, opaque muddy water tinted deep red that pulses slightly with bubbles on the surface, twisted dead trees with brittle branches, red moss and tall roots rising from the flooded mud, narrow safe paths made of stepping stones, fallen logs and rotten wooden boardwalks crossing the swamp, a dark river with a broken wooden footbridge. Difficult terrain everywhere. + estilo

## 3. Portão de Saída (Saída)

> A small open clearing of natural vegetation ending at the arena's outer brick wall, where a huge crude exit gate made of rusted metal sheets and welded scrap iron stands shut, extremely solid; next to the gate a small generator bolted to the brick wall with a big lever; tall grass, bushes and a few trees around the clearing, floodlights mounted on the wall. + estilo

## 4. Casa de Máquinas (Desafio)

> A corrupted, neglected industrial shed with walls of rusty corrugated sheets stained with dark dried blood, illegible graffiti and torn faded warning signs (electric shock, toxic materials) on sliding doors, broken pocked concrete floor; inside, a big boiler room with machines, pipes, valves and gauges, a corroded metal ladder leading to a raised boiler control room with control panels, cables on the floor, puddles of dark oil and blood, red emergency lights. + estilo

## 5. Torre de Vigia (Passagem)

> A rusty watchtower of metal and rotten wood standing in a clearing, surrounded by two torn military tents that once formed an abandoned checkpoint, a wrecked burned military jeep, sections of fallen barbed-wire fences, large boulders giving cover, scattered vegetation, rust and dried blood on the structures and on the ground. + estilo

## 6. Ruínas (Passagem)

> Overgrown stone ruins of an old building, broken stones and collapsed walls scattered around creating obstacles to walk between them, vegetation and roots invading everything; at the center, the best-preserved part of the ruins with an ancient stone sacrificial altar marked by a large dried blood stain, candles melted on the stones, a feeling of many past blood sacrifices. + estilo

## 7. Cabana do Caçador (Passagem, Escotilha Principal)

> An isolated hunter's cabin of dark, damp, aged wood with a degraded roof and small wooden windows, in a forest clearing invaded by dense vegetation; inside a single room with a table, shelves of canned food, hunting trophies and an axe; at the back a creaking wooden staircase going down to a basement used as a dormitory with old foam mattresses, empty cans, food scraps and dirt; outside on the ground, a round heavy metal hatch leading to underground tunnels. + estilo

## 8. Poço e Gazebo (Passagem)

> Open uneven land with small ravines, piles of stones and spread-out bushes, small sparse trees; an old round well of worn stone with a rusty bucket on a frayed rope and a rotten unstable wooden pulley frame; hidden behind rocks and trees, a small gazebo with four dark wooden pillars and a conical roof, relatively clean, a few roof tiles broken or missing. + estilo

## 9. Abatedouro (Desafio)

> The ruins of an old stone fort partially hidden by dense vegetation, roots and vines growing through cracked stone walls, stone stairways up to the walls; inside, an opening in the floor flanked by two statues with faces destroyed by deep claw marks, marking a dark worn spiral staircase going down into the dungeon. Below: a damp underground corridor with stone pillars and small cells carved into the rock with rusty bars, scratch marks and blood; a large slaughter room crowded with cages, rough wooden tables full of scratches, chairs with leather straps, chains and big meat hooks hanging from the ceiling, melted candles, a big stone table covered in blood and rusty torture tools; at the back a circular chamber with a pool of blood in the center, a high ceiling with a barred opening to the sky, torch holders on the walls and upside-down bodies hanging from hooks dripping into the pool. + estilo

## 10. Portão de Saída (Saída)

> Mesmo prompt da zona 3. Pra ficar diferente, acrescente: "a second exit gate on the opposite side of the arena, with a few abandoned supplies and a broken spotlight near the wall".

## 11. Acampamento (Passagem)

> An isolated campsite in a clearing surrounded by dense, silent native forest, packed dirt ground with shallow puddles of reddish liquid that never dry, a central stone fire pit burned down to fine ashes with big logs around it used as benches; a large blue tent with comfortable pillows and a thick duvet inside, a yellow tent still standing but torn open on one side, and a small dark green one-person tent with a worn book, a thin sheet and a plush toy inside. Tense, too quiet. + estilo

## 12. Posto Médico (Desafio)

> A small deteriorated, abandoned medical post among the vegetation, blood stains on walls and floor, rubble and medical waste; a reception with a long chipped wooden counter, a broken computer on the floor, scattered papers and worn chairs, some overturned; a corridor to an infirmary, a long room with rows of rusty metal beds, moldy torn mattresses and old stained sheets, damp walls scribbled with drawings of blood and veins that grow toward the morgue; a cold morgue with white tiles stained dark red, blood veins spreading over the walls and floor like a living organism, and one wall covered by metal body drawers. + estilo

## Extra: Túneis sob a Arena

> A network of narrow underground maintenance tunnels and sewers beneath the arena, rusty industrial pipes along the brick walls, metal ladders up to round hatches in the ceiling, shallow flowing blood and sewage on the floor, toxic green-red gas leaking from cracked pipes, rats and insects, a prison cell with rusty bars at one end. + estilo
